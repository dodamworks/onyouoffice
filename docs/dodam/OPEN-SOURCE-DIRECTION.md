# Project policy

Onyou Office is a free desktop application developed under Apache-2.0.
AI services are optional and have separate provider pricing, subscriptions,
limits and policies. No account is required merely to build the app or run
mocked-provider tests.

Keep the GenOffice → IeumOffice → Onyou Office lineage visible. Preserve original
LICENSE/NOTICE, authors, dependency/font notices and source acknowledgements.
Do not present inherited document engines as independently authored Onyou work.

Contributions are accepted under the existing project license, subject to any
third-party notices. Brand use is separate; see [TRADEMARKS.md](../../TRADEMARKS.md).
There is no separate CLA in the current contribution process.

Prefer small reproducible fixes, synthetic samples and focused reviews.
Community review helps maintenance but does not replace release validation.
Do not publish personal data, credentials, customer documents or account/profile
configuration. Use [private security reporting](../../SECURITY.md) when needed.

Publication, signed installers, update delivery and any future hosted AI service
are separate operations. None is enabled by a source contribution or CI run.
