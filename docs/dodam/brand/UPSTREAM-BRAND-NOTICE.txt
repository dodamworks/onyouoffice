IeumOffice brand artwork
The IeumOffice app icon, logo lockup and AI button mark are original
compositions for this project: the sources in apps/shell/build/brand, the icon
and logo files tools/gen-brand-assets.mjs derives from them, and
packages/ui/src/AiMark.tsx. Their geometry is adapted from the Lucide icon set
(https://lucide.dev), icons `circle`, `square`, `link` and `message-square`.
